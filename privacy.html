Privacy Policy — Cho Rack

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Contact: laplaiall4@gmail.com

Cho Rack ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that replaces broken social-media link previews with working embeds. This policy explains what data the Bot processes and stores, and the choices you have.

1. What the Bot does with messages

When a message posted in a server channel contains a link to a supported platform (X/Twitter, Bluesky, Pixiv, Twitch, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Facebook), the Bot reads the message content in memory to detect the link, hides the original preview, and posts a fixed preview.

The Bot does not store message content. Message text is processed in memory only and is never written to a database or log.

2. Data the Bot stores

The Bot does not collect: message content, direct messages (the Bot does not operate in DMs), email addresses, real names, or payment data.

3. Third parties

To build a fixed preview, the URL you posted is sent to the relevant platform or embed service (for example the Bluesky public API, FxEmbed, vxTwitter, phixiv, TikWM, or similar services listed in the Bot's documentation). Those services receive the link — the same information anyone clicking the link would reveal. No Discord user data is sold or shared for advertising, ever.

4. Your choices

  • Run /ignore-me at any time to make the Bot skip your links completely.
  • Server administrators can disable the Bot per platform or per channel with /settings, or remove the Bot from the server entirely.
  • To request deletion of stored data about you (opt-out record, fix-log entries), email laplaiall4@gmail.com with your Discord user ID. Requests are handled within 30 days.

5. Server removal

When the Bot is removed from a server, that server's settings are marked for deletion and permanently erased after 30 days.

6. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly store data about them beyond the Discord IDs described above, which Discord itself controls under its own terms.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the Bot's support channel and by updating the effective date above.
